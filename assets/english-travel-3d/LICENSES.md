# 3D Asset Licenses

The 3D models in this directory are selected assets from the Kenney packs listed below. Each source pack is released under the [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) public-domain dedication.

Kenney states that the assets may be used in personal, educational, and commercial projects and that attribution is not required. Optional credit may be written as `Kenney`. See the [Kenney support and licensing FAQ](https://kenney.nl/support) for details.

## Local asset layout

Only selected GLB models from the source packs are included here; this directory does not contain complete copies of the original Kenney archives.

```text
models/
├── characters/  # Mini Characters
├── city/        # City Kit (Commercial)
├── roads/       # City Kit (Roads)
├── furniture/   # Furniture Kit
├── market/      # Mini Market

```
